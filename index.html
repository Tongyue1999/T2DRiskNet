使用成人2型糖尿病患者的纵向临床记录，研究首次心血管事件风险，以及有限复查名额下的患者优先排序。

Study first cardiovascular event risk in adults with type 2 diabetes using longitudinal clinical records, and prioritise additional reviews under limited capacity.

患者风险研究

Patient risk research

本地工作台支持诊断、用药、检验等事件序列，提供3、6、12、36、60个月时间窗。输入仅限评估日及之前的资料。

The local workbench accepts diagnosis, medication and laboratory histories with 3, 6, 12, 36 and 60 month horizons. Predictions use only information available at assessment.

复查容量比较

Review capacity comparison

研究最高风险5%、10%、20%人群的事件捕获率、阳性预测值和漏识别率。策略比较需要真实结局及可用随访。

Evaluate event capture, positive predictive value and missed events at top-risk 5%, 10% and 20% capacities. Strategy comparison requires observed outcomes and usable follow-up.

医院资料接入

Hospital data integration

支持本地文件关联与资料质量核查。人口学资料、结局和模型身份须核实后，才可用于正式验证。

Supports local file linkage and data quality checks. Demographics, outcomes and model identities require verification before formal validation.

当前公开版本能做什么

What this public version provides

点击“在线运行模型与仿真”，可使用合成病例运行两个真实模型、比较5%／10%／20%复查容量，并在模拟医生审核后下载中英文患者报告。合成结局仅演示计算流程，不能证明模型优越或预防事件。

Open the online workbench to run two real models on synthetic cases, compare 5%/10%/20% review capacities, and download Chinese and English patient reports after simulated clinician review. Invented outcomes demonstrate the workflow, not superiority or events prevented.

模型权重保存在私有运行仓库，医院原始数据、患者病历和逐患者结果未公开。公开演示不接收真实患者资料，不连接医院系统。

Model weights remain in a private runtime repository. Original hospital data, patient notes and patient-level results are not published. The public demo accepts no real patient records and is not connected to hospital systems.

临床行动与证据边界

Clinical action and evidence

行动是额外评估、强化复诊或管理复核。未被优先选中者继续指南推荐治疗。捕获未来事件不等于预防事件；药物归因不能证明个体治疗效果。当前研究原型尚未证明临床获益。

The action is additional assessment, closer follow-up or management review. Guideline-recommended treatment continues regardless of prioritisation. Event capture does not establish prevention, and medication attribution does not establish individual treatment effects. Clinical benefit has not been established by this research prototype.
